- Flowers/gardens: falling shaped petals; forest leaves have veins and fireflies.

- Chocolate/fountains/ocean/yacht/swan/whale: liquid motion, droplets and expanding ripples.
- Phoenix/peacock, butterfly and wildlife: flame, iridescent glints, flutter, forest or snowy environments matching the subject.
- Castles/palaces/temple: light beams and glints, with moon/stars/clouds where named.
- Vehicles: speed trails and sky clouds for aircraft; original vehicle movies remain visible.
- Aurora/space/galaxy/meteors/lanterns: moving stars, orbital rings, aurora curtains, streaking meteors or floating lit lanterns.
